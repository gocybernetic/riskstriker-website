What to establish before an assessment.

  1. The agent, use case and actions it can take.
  2. Your risk tolerance, red lines and required failure behaviour.
  3. Authorised test targets, synthetic data and environment representation.
  4. How each material consequence will be observed.
  5. Who reviews the evidence and accepts residual risk.

These are proposed assessment requirements. This page makes no claim of government endorsement, accreditation, contract award or current pilot deployment.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE DOES NOT SAY

Tested does not mean universally safe.

An assessment does not certify an agent or guarantee future behaviour. Coverage, unresolved failures, inconclusive results and simulated components remain part of the conclusion. Risk acceptance stays with the customer.

Initial scope excludes production runtime defence, a kill switch and automatic remediation.
