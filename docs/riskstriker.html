RISKSTRIKER / PLATFORM IN DEVELOPMENT

What can the agent do when things go wrong?

We're building RiskStriker to test consequential AI agents in context, measure downstream impact and verify whether controls contain it.

THE METHOD

Context. Failure.
Consequence. Control.

The proposed assessment follows the complete system, from the agent's authority to the actions that reach downstream services.

01 / DEFINE

Make the boundary testable.

Identify tools, permissions, data and autonomy. Define the organisation's risk tolerance and red lines. Decide how each material consequence will be observed.

02 / CHALLENGE

Introduce a realistic failure.

Exercise cyber, operational and agentic scenarios in an authorised test environment. Separate attempted actions from realised impact.

03 / REPEAT

Test the control change.

After customer remediation, restore the starting conditions and rerun the same scenario. Record what changed and what remains unresolved.

THE PLANNED OUTPUT

Tested Operating
Conditions.

A record of the configuration, autonomy and controls under which the assessed agent stayed within stated risk tolerance across the scenarios actually tested.

Independent control verification refers to the controls being assessed. It does not mean independence from Cybernetic Limited or accredited certification.

THE LIMIT OF THE CLAIM

A test result is evidence. It is not a guarantee.

Results apply to the configuration, environment and scenarios assessed. Untested areas, unresolved failures and inconclusive results belong in the conclusion. The organisation remains responsible for risk acceptance.

RiskStriker is in development. Initial scope excludes production runtime defence, automatic remediation and certification.
