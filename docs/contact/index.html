EVALUATION PREPARATION

Start with a specific agent and use case.

A useful assurance discussion begins with the actions an agent can take, the controls around it and the consequences your organisation needs to avoid.

Prepare your evaluation context.

  1. Describe the agent’s purpose and operational owner.
  2. List the systems, data and actions within its reach.
  3. Identify the risk tolerance and red lines that matter.
  4. Describe the authorised test environment and available observations.
